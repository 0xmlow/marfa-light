Export a token as GLB

Builds the token's world at a chosen Marfa minute (April 17, 2027) and saves it as binary glTF for Blender, Cinema 4D, a viewer or AR. The sky, stars and moon are shaders and are left out; the sun comes along as a directional light and the hero view as a camera. Instanced plants, rocks and flowers are merged into ordinary meshes. Clock only keeps just the clock, standing at the origin.
