2. Apple Watch

Photos face any watch, two minutes

Four stills of today's sun: dawn, midday, golden hour and night. Your watch draws the real time over them.

  1. Open the stills on your iPhone and save them to a new album in Photos.
  2. In the Watch app on the iPhone, open Face Gallery and choose Photos.
  3. Under Content, pick that album, then tap Add.

Marfa Light watch app moving hands

A full day in 288 frames, one every five minutes. The app shows the frame for the minute you are in, so the hands, sun and shadows keep time on your wrist. About 11 MB, two or three minutes to make.

The pack goes into the watch app's Frames folder. Re-make it every month or so: the sun moves with the seasons.
